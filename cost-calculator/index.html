What Will My Lifestyle Cost?

Understanding Budget Levels:

These estimates are relative to your current cost of living, not absolute dollar amounts.

  • Frugal: Living below the local average. Cooking at home, free activities, modest accommodations.
  • Moderate: Living at the local average. Mix of eating out and cooking, standard activities, comfortable accommodations.
  • Comfortable: Living above the local average. Regular dining out, premium activities, nicer accommodations.

Slow Travel Costs Explained:

Estimates include short-term rentals (Airbnb/furnished apartments), travel insurance, local transportation, activities, and flights between countries if traveling to multiple destinations.

Snowbird Costs Explained:

Home situation refers to your primary/summer home (where you spend ~7 months). Winter destination costs are for your warm-weather rental (~5 months). The calculator shows your total year-round costs averaged monthly.

RV Costs Explained:

Estimates include RV payment/rental (if applicable), fuel, campground fees, insurance, maintenance, food, healthcare, and entertainment. Does NOT include initial RV purchase price.
