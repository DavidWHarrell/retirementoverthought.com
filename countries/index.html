How to Use This Tool

Click any column header to sort by that metric. Click again to reverse the sort order.

Use the search box to filter countries by name or region.

All scores are normalized to 0-100 scale, with 100 being the best. Rankings are compiled from multiple independent sources (see Sources section below).

What the Columns Mean

Overall Score: Weighted average of all factors
Healthcare: Quality, accessibility, and cost of medical care
Cost of Living: Relative affordability for housing, food, services (higher = cheaper)
Safety: Crime rates, political stability, personal security
Quality of Life: Infrastructure, climate, culture, amenities
Ease of Entry: Visa requirements, residency process, language barriers
Expat Community: Size and integration of expat population
