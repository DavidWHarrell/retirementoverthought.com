🏥 Healthcare Foundation (Under 65)

Your #1 Priority: Healthcare costs before Medicare eligibility can make or break early retirement plans. Master the ACA marketplace.
Calculate your true annual healthcare costs
Include premiums, deductibles, out-of-pocket maximums, prescriptions. Budget $8K-$24K/year for couple depending on age and health.
Master the ACA (Affordable Care Act) marketplace
Healthcare.gov is your primary solution. Understanding subsidy optimization can save $10K+ per year.
ACA Subsidy Optimization Strategy
  • Income Sweet Spot: Keep MAGI between 150-250% of Federal Poverty Level
  • For 2025: Single $22,590-$37,650, Couple $30,660-$51,100
  • Tactics: Time capital gains, strategic Roth conversions, harvest losses, control dividends
  • Result: Reduce $1,500/month premiums to $200/month with proper income management
Design retirement income to optimize ACA subsidies
Control MAGI through strategic withdrawals from different account types. Use Roth for large expenses (doesn't count as income).
Choose between Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum ACA plans
Bronze = low premiums/high deductibles. Silver often best value with subsidies. Gold if ongoing medical needs.
Maximize Health Savings Account (HSA) if eligible
If on High-Deductible Health Plan, HSA is triple-tax-advantaged. Max contribution: $4,300 individual, $8,550 family (2025).
Plan for the "gap years" (ages 60-65)
Most expensive healthcare years - premiums peak but Medicare hasn't kicked in. Budget extra 20-30% for this period.

🚐 RV Living Healthcare

RV Pro Tip: Your domicile state choice determines ALL your healthcare options. Choose carefully before hitting the road.
Establish legal domicile in RV-friendly state
Popular: South Dakota, Florida, Texas. Your healthcare options depend entirely on domicile state - this is foundational.
State Comparison
  • Florida: BCBS "Blue Card" offers nationwide PPO network - excellent for RVers
  • Texas: ACA plans often HMO-only in popular domicile areas (Livingston)
  • South Dakota: Easy residency, but limited ACA marketplace options
Choose nationwide coverage (if under 65)
Many ACA plans only cover in-state. Look for PPO plans or Florida Blue's "Blue Card" network that works nationwide.
If 65+: Original Medicare + Medigap is perfect for RVers
Works anywhere in US, no networks. Plan G or Plan N recommended. AVOID Medicare Advantage - network restrictions don't work for travelers.
Consider telemedicine services as backup
Services like Teladoc provide 24/7 doctor access from your RV. Not insurance replacement but useful for minor issues.
Plan prescription medication management
Use mail-order pharmacies, 90-day supplies, GoodRx for cash pricing. Research pharmacy chains with nationwide locations.

🌍 Expat & Slow Travel Healthcare

Expat Pro Tip: Medicare does NOT cover healthcare outside the US. You need separate international insurance or local coverage.
Understand Medicare doesn't work abroad
Original Medicare covers zero healthcare costs outside US (except rare exceptions near Canada/Mexico border).
Decide whether to keep paying Medicare Part B premiums
If you drop Part B while abroad, face 10% lifetime penalty when re-enrolling. Only drop if certain you won't return to US.
Research international health insurance options
Major providers: Cigna Global, Allianz Care, GeoBlue. Use brokers like Expat Insurance or International Citizens Insurance for quotes.
What to Look For
  • Coverage area (worldwide vs regional vs excluding USA)
  • Pre-existing condition coverage
  • Medical evacuation/repatriation
  • Annual maximum ($1M+ recommended)
  • Age limits (some cut off at 65-70)
Investigate local healthcare systems in destination
Many countries let foreign residents buy into national health (Spain, Portugal, Thailand). Often much cheaper than US.
Get Medigap plan with foreign travel coverage (if 65+)
Plans G, M, N cover 80% of emergency care abroad (first 60 days, $50K lifetime max, $250 deductible).
Consider geographic arbitrage for healthcare savings
Living abroad where healthcare is 70% cheaper can dramatically reduce costs. Mexico, Portugal, Thailand, Colombia popular.

🦅 Snowbird Healthcare (Two Locations)

Snowbird Tip: Coordinate healthcare between both locations. Original Medicare (65+) makes this seamless.
If 65+: Original Medicare is perfect for snowbirds
Works nationwide, no networks. See any doctor/hospital accepting Medicare in both locations.
Avoid Medicare Advantage if snowbirding between states
Provider networks usually don't extend to winter location. You'll pay out-of-network or have no coverage.
Establish primary care providers in both locations
Build relationships with doctors in summer and winter locations. Schedule annual checkups accordingly.
Coordinate prescription refills with travel schedule
Get 90-day supplies, use mail-order, or transfer prescriptions to pharmacy chain in both locations.
If snowbirding internationally (Mexico, etc.)
Get Medigap with foreign travel coverage OR separate travel medical insurance. Consider local healthcare (often very affordable).

🚢 Cruise Ship Living Healthcare

Critical: Ship medical facilities are for emergencies only. You MUST have robust evacuation coverage - this is non-negotiable.
Understand ship medical facilities are extremely limited
Ships have small urgent care centers - NOT hospitals. Can't handle surgery, advanced diagnostics, complex procedures.
Get comprehensive medical evacuation insurance
Medical evacuation from ship can cost $100K-$300K. Being airlifted to hospital is common. Minimum coverage: $250K medical, $100K evacuation.
Evacuation Coverage Options
  • Travel Insurance: Allianz, Travel Guard, World Nomads
  • Annual Plans: If cruising frequently, annual plan may be cheaper
  • Check cruise line requirements - some mandate insurance
Medicare does NOT work on cruise ships
Medicare only covers ships within 6 hours of US port AND only emergencies. Most cruise time not covered.
Maintain US-based health insurance as foundation
Keep ACA plan (under 65) or Medicare (65+) for US ports and if evacuated back to US.
Plan healthcare visits during port stops
Schedule routine checkups, dental, prescriptions during US port stops or when visiting home between cruises.
Stock 90-180 day prescription supplies
Ship pharmacies expensive and limited inventory. Get maximum prescriptions before boarding, plan refills around ports.
Verify travel insurance covers "continuous cruising"
Some policies limit consecutive travel days (60-90 days). Need policy allowing continuous ship living.

📋 Medicare Planning (Age 65+)

Critical Timing: Missing enrollment windows results in lifetime penalties. Mark these dates on your calendar.
Understand Initial Enrollment Period (IEP)
3 months before 65th birthday, birthday month, 3 months after (7 months total). Missing this = lifetime penalties.
Penalties for Late Enrollment
  • Part B: 10% higher premiums for every 12 months delayed - FOR LIFE
  • Part D: 1% of national base premium per month delayed - FOR LIFE
  • These add up significantly over retirement
Enroll in Medicare Part A (Hospital Insurance)
Free for most people if you or spouse worked 10+ years. Covers hospital stays, skilled nursing, hospice.
Enroll in Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance)
Covers doctors, outpatient care, preventive services. Standard premium $185/month (2025), higher if income over $106K.
Shop for Medigap during 6-month open enrollment
Starting when you turn 65 and enroll in Part B. Insurers CANNOT deny you or charge more for pre-existing conditions during this window.
Choose the right Medigap plan for your lifestyle
Plan G most comprehensive. Plan N lower premiums with small copays. All Medigap works nationwide - perfect for travelers.
Enroll in Medicare Part D (Prescription Drug Coverage)
Compare plans annually - coverage changes yearly. Use Medicare.gov plan finder with your medication list.

⏰ Approaching Medicare Age (60-64)

Transition Planning: These are the most expensive pre-Medicare years. Plan your transition carefully.
Continue maximizing ACA subsidies until 65
Keep managing income strategically. These years have highest pre-Medicare premiums - subsidies critical.
Create Medicare enrollment calendar
Mark Initial Enrollment Period dates. Set reminders for 3 months before 65th birthday to start process.
Research Medigap plans before turning 65
Start shopping 6 months early. Know which plan you'll choose so you can enroll immediately at 65.
Budget for Medicare Part B premiums in retirement income
$185/month base (2025), but increases with income. IRMAA surcharges start at $106K single, $212K couple.
Plan final Roth conversions before Medicare
IRMAA charges based on income 2 years prior. Do large conversions before age 63 to avoid surcharges at 65.

🏥 Managing Chronic Health Conditions

Prioritize comprehensive coverage over cost
Don't choose high-deductible catastrophic plans. Need coverage for specialists, ongoing care, procedures.
Verify specialists are in-network before choosing plan
If seeing specialists regularly, confirm they accept your plan. Out-of-network costs can be devastating.
If traveling with chronic conditions: establish care in both locations
Find specialists in travel destinations. Get medical records digitized for easy sharing between providers.
Research international coverage for pre-existing conditions
Many international plans exclude pre-existing conditions. Look for plans specifically covering chronic conditions (Cigna Global, Allianz Care).

💊 Managing Expensive Prescriptions

Check drug formularies before choosing any plan
Use Medicare.gov plan finder (Part D) or check ACA plan documents. Some drugs only covered by certain plans - can save thousands.
Investigate manufacturer assistance programs
Many drug manufacturers offer copay assistance or patient assistance programs. Can dramatically reduce costs.
Consider mail-order pharmacies for maintenance medications
Often cheaper for 90-day supplies. Works well for travelers - can ship ahead or to mail forwarding service.
Research international prescription costs
Many medications 60-80% cheaper abroad. Canada, Mexico, India common sources. Understand legality and safety.
Use GoodRx for price comparison
Sometimes cash price with GoodRx coupon cheaper than insurance copay. Always check before filling.

💰 Healthcare + Roth Conversion Strategy

The Balancing Act: Roth conversions increase MAGI, reducing ACA subsidies. Strategic timing is everything.
Understand Roth conversions reduce ACA subsidies
Conversion income counts toward MAGI. Converting too much can eliminate subsidies, costing more than tax savings.
Calculate the subsidy cliff (400% FPL)
At 400% FPL (~$60K single, $81K couple), subsidies disappear completely. Don't accidentally cross this converting.
Do conversions in strategic years
Convert in years with lower expenses or when living abroad (if not claiming ACA). Pause conversions in high-subsidy years.
Plan final conversions before age 63
Medicare IRMAA charges based on income 2 years prior. Large conversions at 63+ can cause surcharges at 65+.
Model the tradeoff: tax savings vs subsidy loss
Use spreadsheet or financial planner. Sometimes better to accept higher taxes and keep subsidies.

🎖️ Using VA Healthcare Benefits

Verify VA healthcare eligibility
Check eligibility at VA.gov. Based on service history, discharge status, disability rating, income.
Understand VA coverage is nationwide
VA facilities available across US. Can receive care at any VA location - perfect for travelers.
Decide how to coordinate VA with Medicare (if 65+)
VA + Medicare work together. Use VA for services it covers well, Medicare for others. Both valid strategies.
Research VA coverage abroad (limited)
VA only covers care abroad in very specific circumstances. If traveling internationally, need supplemental coverage.

📅 Annual Healthcare Review

Review Medicare Part D during AEP (Oct 15 - Dec 7)
Plans change coverage and costs annually. Always shop around - last year's best plan may not be this year's.
Review ACA plans during open enrollment (Nov 1 - Jan 15)
Plans, premiums, subsidies change yearly. Always compare - may find better coverage or lower cost.
Re-optimize income strategy for subsidies
Income needs change yearly. Recalculate optimal MAGI, plan Roth conversions, time capital gains.
Update medication list and verify coverage
If medications changed, optimal Part D plan may have changed. Check formularies for all current prescriptions.
Reassess travel plans and insurance needs
Traveling more/less this year? Time abroad changing? Adjust coverage accordingly.
